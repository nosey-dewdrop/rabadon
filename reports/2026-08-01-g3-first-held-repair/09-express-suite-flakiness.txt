Why it matters to the product:
  the arbiter is exactly as deterministic as the project's own suite. A flaky
  suite costs rabadon RECALL, never SOUNDNESS:
    - false REPAIR_FAIL  possible  -> a good fix is thrown away, tree untouched
    - false REPAIR_OK    not possible from flake alone -> green still requires the
      real bug to be gone AND all 91 locks intact
  fail-closed is the right side to be wrong on, and it is the side we are on.

This is what run 07a was: the cheat had landed, but the arbiter's re-run hit the
flake (exit 1) and the fake was rejected as "still red" instead of as tamper.
Right verdict, WRONG reason, and no output to tell the two apart. That blindness
is what commit a65921b fixed; the very next run (07b) named the tampered file and
printed both hashes.
